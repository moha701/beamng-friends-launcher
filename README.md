# BeamNG Friends Launcher: BeamNG.drive multiplayer with your friends

**Play BeamNG.drive online with friends. No server to rent, no account.** One friend hosts on their own PC, the others paste an invite, and the launcher downloads the host's map and mods, starts BeamNG.drive and puts everyone in the same world.

### [⬇ Download the latest version](https://github.com/moha701/beamng-friends-launcher/releases/latest)

Download `BeamNG-Friends-Launcher-<version>-Setup.exe` from the latest release and run it. Windows only.

---

## Features

**Driving together**
- Up to **20 players** in one session (you and 19 friends)
- Smooth friends' cars that move with real physics, even at high speed
- **Damage sync:** when a friend crashes, their car bends and breaks the same way on every screen
- Lights, indicators, hazards, horn, gears and engine follow your friends
- Trailers, resets and teleports show up correctly for everyone
- Everyone gets the host's **time of day and weather**
- Names above cars, and a player list in the game (**F4**) with distance and ping

**Hosting and joining**
- **No dedicated server:** whoever hosts runs the session on their own PC
- **Mod sharing:** friends get the host's map and mods automatically, and mods they already have are never downloaded again
- **Invites and Allow button:** nobody joins until the host lets them in. Remember friends' PCs so they come straight in next time
- Find sessions on your Wi-Fi without an invite
- Automatic reconnect after a short internet drop, and **Rejoin** after a game crash
- Help for CGNAT and blocked routers (IPv6, Radmin VPN, Tailscale, ZeroTier)

**Voice and chat**
- **3D proximity voice chat:** voices come from your friends' cars, get quieter and echo with distance, or use radio mode to hear everyone the same
- Push-to-talk or voice activity, per-friend volume, mute
- Chat in the launcher and inside the game (press **T**)

**Light on your PC**
- The in-game part costs about 0.2 to 0.3 ms per frame, even with 19 friends
- Far-away cars pause to save FPS, and there is a **Low-end PC mode** for slower computers
- Voice chat runs in the launcher, not in the game

**Always up to date**
- The launcher updates itself in the background. Every update is signed by the developer, so only real updates install
- When BeamNG.drive updates, anything that breaks turns itself off instead of stopping the session, and fixes reach every player automatically

**English and Arabic**, with right-to-left layout.

## How to play

1. **Everyone** installs the launcher and has their own copy of BeamNG.drive (the same version).
2. **The host** opens *Host*, picks a map, chooses which mods to share and presses *Start hosting*. Then they copy the invite and send it to friends.
3. **Friends** paste the invite on *Join* and press *Join*. They see the map and every mod first, then press *Download and join*.
4. The host presses *Allow* when a friend asks to join. That's it, you're driving together.

## Questions

### Can you play BeamNG.drive multiplayer?
Yes. With this launcher you play BeamNG.drive multiplayer on PC: host a session on your PC, send the invite, and your friends drive in the same world as you.

### How do I play BeamNG online with friends?
Install the launcher, host or join with an invite, and press *Allow* when a friend asks to join. See *How to play* above. It works over the internet and on LAN or Wi-Fi.

### Is there BeamNG co-op or free roam with friends?
Yes. Everyone drives together in free roam on any map, including map mods, with crashes, damage, trailers, time and weather shared.

### Do I need a BeamNG multiplayer server?
No. The host's own PC is the server. There is nothing to rent or set up.

### How is this different from BeamMP?
BeamMP is another multiplayer mod with public servers. BeamNG Friends Launcher is made for playing with your own friends: no server and no account, one invite, mods sent automatically, and 3D voice chat built in.

### Windows says "Windows protected your PC".
That happens with new programs from small developers. Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*. Updates after that install by themselves.

### My friend can't reach me.
Open *Settings → Troubleshooting* in the launcher. If your internet provider uses CGNAT (a shared address), the launcher shows the free fixes step by step: IPv6, or a VPN such as Radmin VPN, Tailscale or ZeroTier.

### Do my friends need the same mods?
No. The launcher sends them the host's map and mods automatically.

### Is it free?
Yes. If you like it, there is a *Support the developer* button in the app.

### Does it collect my data?
No account, no analytics and no developer server. Sessions go straight between the host and their friends.
